What we collect when you buy

Your name, email, phone number, delivery and billing address, and what you ordered. We use it to deliver your order, handle returns and complaints, and keep the accounting records Swedish law requires (seven years). The legal basis is our contract with you and our legal obligations.

Who we share it with

Stripe processes your payment, and Klarna if you choose to pay with Klarna. PostNord receives your name, address and phone number to deliver your parcel. Our website is hosted by Cloudflare. We don't sell your data.

Cookies

The shop only stores your cart in your own browser. Stripe uses necessary cookies during checkout to prevent fraud. We don't use advertising cookies.

Garment photo galleries

Each spår garment has its own photo gallery at sparclothing.se/g/ followed by the garment's code. When you claim a garment with the code on its inside label, we store the photos you add, the gallery name you choose and a sign-in cookie on your phone. When someone else sends a photo to your gallery, we store the photo and the name they give (optional) until you approve or decline it. Declined photos are deleted.

We remove location and camera data (EXIF and GPS) from every photo. Galleries are private unless the owner makes them public. The legal basis is consent: yours when you add a photo, and the owner's choices for their gallery. The "Find my photos" feature reads QR codes in the photos you pick on your own phone; photos without your garment's code never leave your phone.

Photos are stored with Cloudflare in the EU. The owner can delete any photo at any time, and handing a garment on deletes its whole gallery. Photos stay until they are deleted. If a photo of you is in someone's gallery and you want it removed, email us with the garment code and we'll delete it.

The gallery sets one necessary cookie for each garment you've claimed, so your phone remembers that you're the owner. It also keeps your list of garments, and the photos you've opened, in your own browser so they open without signal.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se).
