How the code works

1

Your garment, your ID

The QR code on your piece opens a short link like sparclothing.se/g/7K3QXM. No two garments share one.

2

Claim it with the label code

A private code is printed on the inside label. Enter it once and the gallery is yours. Galleries start private.

3

Friends scan and add photos

Anyone who snaps you can scan and upload from their phone's browser. You approve what goes in.

Questions

Where do you ship?

Sweden and the rest of the EU. Orders ship within two working days. Shipping in Sweden is free over 800 kr, otherwise 59 kr. EU shipping is 99 kr.

Can I return my order?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return unworn items. Your garment's gallery is reset before it is resold.

Who can see my photos?

Only you, until you choose otherwise. You can make a gallery link-only or public, remove any photo, and delete everything on request. Location data is stripped from every upload.

What if I sell or give the garment away?

Release it from your account. The next owner claims it with a fresh code and starts with an empty gallery.

Does the QR survive the wash?

The code is screen printed with high contrast and extra error correction. Wash inside out at 30°C and it stays scannable.
